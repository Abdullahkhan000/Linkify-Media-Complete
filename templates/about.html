Why Linkify?

Media data should feel connected, not fragmented.

Building a media product often means integrating multiple services, normalising inconsistent responses, and maintaining separate links for every provider. Linkify Media brings that work behind one developer-friendly interface.

From a single search request, your application can receive structured titles, release data, genres, ratings, cast information, posters, and discovery links in a consistent response.
