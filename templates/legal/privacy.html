1

Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Linkify Media website, dashboard, API services, and related support interactions. It describes our handling of information when you create an account, use our API, contact support, or browse the service.

2

Information we collect

Account information

Email address, name, password credentials, account preferences, subscription tier, and account identifiers.

API activity

API key identifiers, endpoint paths, timestamps, response status, and usage counts used for quotas and diagnostics.

Support information

Information you choose to include when contacting support, such as your email, issue description, and technical context.

Technical information

Basic browser, device, security, and server information may be processed to operate and protect the service.

3

How we use information

We use information to create and maintain accounts, authenticate requests, provide media metadata services, enforce plan limits, measure usage, prevent abuse, send service communications, respond to support requests, process subscriptions, and improve reliability.

We do not use developer API activity to sell personal profiles or expose private account information to other customers.

4

Sharing and third-party providers

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the platform, such as hosting, email delivery, payment processing, authentication, logging, and media metadata providers. These providers receive information needed for their defined service role and are expected to handle it appropriately.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our terms, protect the security of the service, investigate abuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

5

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information we process. Passwords are stored using Django's password hashing system. Access to operational data is limited according to role and service needs.

We retain account and usage information for as long as needed to provide the service, enforce agreements, meet operational requirements, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

Account data

While your account is active and as needed for legitimate records.

Usage logs

Retained for quotas, analytics, security, and troubleshooting.

Support records

Retained as needed to resolve and document support matters.

6

Cookies and sessions

Linkify Media may use essential session technologies to keep you signed in, protect forms, maintain security, and remember necessary service state. We may also use limited operational analytics where configured. You can control cookies through your browser, although disabling essential cookies may affect account functionality.

7

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the processing of your personal information. You may also be able to object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a request, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the scope of your request before responding.

8

Children's privacy

The service is intended for developers and businesses and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers, or legal obligations change. We will update the date at the top of this page and may provide additional notice for material changes where appropriate.
